Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the AR Design publishing application, an internal tool that posts the studio's own videos and photo carousels to its official TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts.

2. What we access

When the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, we receive an access token for that one account, plus basic information needed to publish (such as the account's posting options). We do not access, collect or store data about other TikTok users, their videos, followers or messages.

3. How we use it

Tokens are used only to publish AR Design's own approved content at scheduled times. They are kept in private, access-restricted environment storage and are never shared, sold or used for advertising or analytics.

4. Retention and revocation

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Apps), after which the tokens stop working and are deleted on request.

5. Third parties

We do not share information with third parties, except with TikTok, Meta (Instagram, Facebook) and our hosting providers as needed to publish and run the application.
